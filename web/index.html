Why This Decision

Saved explanations are ODAM maps (one backward pass through the PyTorch model). Live explanations run D-RISE on this browser's model: the part is hidden in random patches many times, and the heatmap shows which visible patches kept the detection alive.

How Good Is It

Automation, escapes and false rejects are cross-fitted: thresholds chosen on one half of the test parts, measured on the other half.
